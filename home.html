Our Heritage & Mission

Breathe Tradition. Choose Sustainability.

At VASDHAVITRA, we believe every fabric deserves a second life. We transform pre-loved sarees and surplus textiles into beautiful handcrafted fabric hand fans, combining traditional charm, creativity, and sustainability.

"A little breeze, a beautiful story, and a greener choice."

Celebrating Indian Textile Heritage

Each fan celebrates the beauty of Indian textiles with unique colours and patterns. Whether you are looking for a stylish everyday accessory or a thoughtful gift for festivals and special occasions, our fans bring together tradition and mindful living.

By giving existing fabrics a new purpose, we aim to encourage eco-conscious choices and contribute to a greener future.

The Upcycling Promise

Sourced from authentic vintage sarees and artisan surplus, every piece saves precious fabric from landfills while supporting generational weavers.
